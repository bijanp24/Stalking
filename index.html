The Severe Criminal Consequences of Online Intimidation

View Legal Brief (PDF)

Under California law, online perpetrators who cross the line into targeted intimidation face catastrophic criminal liabilities. Prosecutors combine multiple statutes to construct high-stakes felony prosecutions designed to secure maximum prison exposure.

I. The Sentencing & Exposure Matrix

CPC § 422(a) (Criminal Threats)

Charged as felonies due to household danger. Each distinct digital threat is a separate, consecutive count. Base exposure: 3 years per count.

CPC § 422.75 ("In Concert" Hate Crime)

If bias-motivated and committed collectively/willfully on forums, it triggers an automatic, consecutive 4-year enhancement per count.

The Multi-Count Cascade

3 Felony Counts + 3 Enhancements
21 Years
Aggregate Maximum State Prison

II. The "Three Strikes" Law

Under CPC § 1192.7(c)(38), Criminal Threats is a "Serious Felony" and a permanent "Felony Strike."

  • Sentence Doubling: Any future felony charge is automatically doubled.
  • 85% Minimum Serve: Strike offenders are federally mandated to serve 85% of their sentence behind bars.

III. Subpoena & Unmasking Reality

Anonymity is rapidly dismantled through standard judicial channels:

  • 1. Platform Subpoenas: Reddit hands over email records and historical IP/MAC logs.
  • 2. ISP Unmasking: IPs are matched by ISPs (Comcast, Spectrum) to physical household billing addresses.
  • 3. Warrants: Digital trails secure search warrants leading directly to physical arrests.

Networked Harassment & Proxy Stalking Architecture

When targeted by "many stalkers," an orchestrator weaponizes third parties and online crowds to overwhelm the victim.

1. Primary Orchestrator (The Nexus)

Often a former intimate partner (35% of persistent stalking cases). Maintains physical and digital distance while directing attack vectors.

2. Weaponization Mechanisms (Doxxing & Manipulation)

Publishes personal identifiers, manipulated photos, or defamatory narratives online to incite outrage or targeted contact from strangers.

3. Secondary Perpetrators ("Proxy Stalkers")

Unwitting or malicious third parties who execute threats, phone calls, or digital swarming. Reduces barrier to entry to a single keystroke.

Impact: Incapacitating Threat Ambiguity

The victim cannot assess the physical proximity or credibility of dozens of anonymous actors, inducing severe psychological terror.

Prosecutorial Burdens (CALCRIM 2701 Four Elements):

  • 1. A court lawfully issued a written protective order.
  • 2. Defendant had knowledge/notice of the order (proof of service).
  • 3. Defendant possessed the ability to follow the order.
  • 4. Defendant willfully violated the order.

PC 273.6 Penalty Escalation

Standard Violation (PC 273.6(a))
Misdemeanor

Up to 1 year in county jail and $1,000 fine.

With Physical Injury (PC 273.6(b))
Enhanced Misdemeanor

Mandatory minimum 30 days in jail; up to $2,000 fine.

Repeat Offense w/ Threat (PC 273.6(d))
Wobbler / Felony

16m, 2y, or 3y State Prison + $10,000 fine (within 7 years).

Mandatory Collateral Consequence
Lifelong Firearm Ban

Any felony conviction under PC 273.6 permanently terminates firearm rights under state/federal law.
