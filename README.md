# Stalking and Cyber Harassment Threat Assessment

This repository serves as a legal and investigative dossier documenting an active physical threat alongside a coordinated cyberbullying campaign.

## Overview
Welcome to the Stalking threat assessment project.

### Summary of the Medico-Legal and Investigative Dossier

The core artifact is a single-page interactive application (`index.html`) that provides a comprehensive, two-pronged legal narrative. It sharply delineates legally verified physical threats from active allegations requiring further investigation.

#### Key Modules & Legal Strategy Integrated:
1. **The Realistic Danger Baseline (Physical Threat):** Establishes the immediate household threat via a verified restraining order against an individual previously imprisoned for Felony Attempted Murder. This baseline validates hyper-vigilance and proves that fears of physical escalation are entirely reasonable.
2. **The Cyberbullying Campaign (Active Allegations):** Documents a coordinated sexual cyberbullying campaign (in violation of 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and platform Terms of Service). 
3. **Probable Cause for Investigation:** Connects the severe online harassment with the verified physical threat baseline to establish probable cause for retaining a Private Investigator to issue subpoenas, unmask IP addresses, and investigate alleged corporate connections (e.g., Riot Games).
